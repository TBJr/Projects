# Code of conduct

This repository welcomes constructive contributions and discussion. Treat other people with respect, give useful feedback, and allow room for different experience levels and perspectives.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, sharing another person's private information, and repeated disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, or other project spaces.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation to keep the project welcoming.

To report a concern privately, use a contact method published at [thomasbrown.app](https://thomasbrown.app/). If no private method is listed, open a brief issue requesting one without posting personal details. Reports will be reviewed by the maintainer; no response time is guaranteed.
